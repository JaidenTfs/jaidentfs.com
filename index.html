About Me

JaidenTfs is a composer for games, film, and interactive media, currently studying music composition at the bachelor's level. His work covers orchestral, chiptune, and cinematic writing, including a short film score, several game jam soundtracks, and the Seawright Line Songbook for Dear Clarent. He's used to tight deadlines, like scoring a full game soundtrack in a 48-hour jam, and likes building music around recurring motifs that follow a story's characters and mood.

Most of the games he's scored are free to play on his itch.io page, and comments there are always welcome.

To talk about a project, the easiest ways to reach him are the contact page or Instagram DMs.
